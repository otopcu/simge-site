## Requirements and samples

SimGe runs on **Windows 10/11 (64-bit)** and requires the **.NET 10 Desktop Runtime (x64)**. See [Installation & Updates](UserManual/Installation.md) for setup and upgrade instructions.

The installer includes the **Chat** and **STMS** sample projects. RPR/NETN research corpora and their reports are not distributed with SimGe. Follow [Quick Start](UserManual/QuickStart.md) to open a bundled sample and save an editable copy.
